# CropGuide Source Registry

## Purpose

This registry records the sources used to curate CropGuide disease records. A record may be added only when a source supports the crop, the disease identity, visible symptoms, or a management step. The app presents photo-based candidates, not laboratory confirmation.

## Core diagnosis rules

Visible symptoms can overlap with insect injury, nutrition, weather, chemicals, and mixed infections. CropGuide must therefore label every result as a possible disease and state when field confirmation or laboratory testing is appropriate. The source material also supports including symptoms, favorable conditions, and management as separate data fields rather than treating a disease name as a complete diagnosis.[1] [2] [3]

## Verified extraction notes

- **Do not infer disease identity from a symptom family alone.** Multiple guides flag root rot, wilt, virus and nutrient or chemical injury as overlapping patterns; a field check belongs with every record.
- **General first response is not a pesticide recommendation.** Source-backed immediate steps are isolation of badly affected material where feasible, sanitation, drainage/leaf-wetness reduction, crop-family rotation, clean planting material, and resistant varieties when locally available.
- **Registered product, active ingredient, rate, timing and PHI depend on country and crop.** The data model therefore records only an instruction to obtain locally registered, label-directed advice after confirmation.

## Record acceptance checklist

| Check | Required outcome |
|---|---|
| Crop fit | The source explicitly covers the crop or its crop group. |
| Disease fit | The disease name and causal group are traceable to a source. |
| Image suitability | At least one allowed symptom is visually observable; otherwise the record is not matched from a photograph alone. |
| Management safety | Guidance begins with sanitation, cultural measures, monitoring, or removal when appropriate. Chemical active-ingredient language is conditional and never substitutes for local registration, label, PPE, or agronomist advice. |
| Confirmation | The record states a practical field check and a trigger for specialist or laboratory confirmation when symptoms overlap. |
| Translation | Arabic, French, and English values must be present before a record is released. |

## Source scope and review status

The expanded catalog uses two explicit provenance levels. A `record_specific` source supports the named crop-and-disease profile and is marked `reviewed`. A `crop_group` source supports a disease family or crop group but is not treated as a record-level confirmation; these rows are marked `source_mapped` and remain in the review queue until a reviewer attaches a crop-specific source. The application must keep presenting all image matches as possible conditions and must never convert a source review into automatic advice changes.

The monthly expanded review checks source-link availability, database completeness, duplicate records, and the source-scope flags. Its output is stored outside version control in `data/review-runs/`; proposed evidence changes must be entered in `data/review-queue.md` and approved by a human before data is rebuilt.
