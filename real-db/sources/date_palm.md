# Date palm (نخيل التمر) — sources, batch of 2026-10-24

10 records, all pages actually opened and read in this run. Note on
counting: the ICARDA guide below is ONE PDF, but it is cited for six
different disease sections, each read separately (section numbers given).
Distinct URLs in this batch: 5 (EPPO ×1, ICARDA guide ×1, UF/IFAS ×3).
Distinct disease sections: 10.

1. Bayoud — https://gd.EPPO.int/taxon/FUSAAL/datasheet
   "EPPO Datasheet: Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. albedinis" — EPPO Global
   Database. Cause: Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. albedinis. EPPO code FUSAAL
   (shown on the opened page).
2. Black scorch — ICARDA guide, Chapter VI §1.2 (see URL below)
   "Date Palm Pests and Diseases Integrated Management Guide" (2018) —
   ICARDA. Cause: Thielaviopsis paradoxa (sexual form Ceratocystis
   paradoxa). Guide also notes Thielaviopsis punctulata reported in
   Qatar/UAE; the record follows the guide's usual agent.
3. Diplodia disease — ICARDA guide, Chapter VI §1.3
   Cause: Diplodia phoenicum, synonym Neodeightonia phoenicum.
4. Graphiola leaf spot — https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/PP140
   "PP-216/PP140: Graphiola Leaf Spot (False Smut) of Palm" — UF/IFAS
   Extension. Cause: Graphiola phoenicis.
5. Khamedj (inflorescence rot) — ICARDA guide, Chapter VI §1.10
   Cause: Mauginiella scaettae (white creamy form; Fusarium moniliforme
   pinkish and Thielaviopsis paradoxa dry brown forms are distinguished
   in the record).
6. Belaat disease — ICARDA guide, Chapter VI §1.7
   Cause: Phytophthora spp., similar to Phytophthora palmivora
   (record uses Phytophthora palmivora per the guide's figure/text).
7. Omphalia root rot — ICARDA guide, Chapter VI §1.11
   Cause: Omphalia tralucida and Omphalia pigmentata (record uses
   O. tralucida as the named species, with O. pigmentata noted).
8. Lethal yellowing — https://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/pp146
   "PP-222/PP146: Lethal Yellowing (LY) of Palm" — UF/IFAS Extension.
   Cause: Candidatus Phytoplasma palmae (16SrIV-A). Date palm
   (Phoenix dactylifera) is shown in the page's Figure 12.
9. Al-Wijam — ICARDA guide, Chapter VI §2.2
   Cause: 16SrI-group phytoplasma, Candidatus Phytoplasma asteris
   (Al-Hudaib et al. 2007), with the guide itself noting a later 16SrII-D
   finding in the same region — the record states this uncertainty
   openly and does not present the agent as settled.
10. Lethal bronzing — https://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/pp163
    "PP243/PP163: Lethal Bronzing Disease (LB)" — UF/IFAS Extension.
    Cause: Candidatus Phytoplasma aculeata. First isolated in Florida
    from edible date palm Phoenix dactylifera.
