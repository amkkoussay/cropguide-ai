source_url is the exact opened page. 9 records, 9 distinct authoritative sources.

1. Stripe rust — https://cropprotectionnetwork.org/encyclopedia/stripe-rust-of-wheat
   "Stripe Rust of Wheat" — Crop Protection Network (multi-state/international
   extension collaboration). Cause: Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici.
2. Leaf rust — https://pnwhandbooks.org/plantdisease/host-disease/wheat-triticum-aestivum-leaf-rust-brown-rust
   "Wheat (Triticum aestivum)-Leaf Rust (Brown Rust)" — Pacific Northwest Pest
   Management Handbooks (rev. March 2026). Cause: Puccinia triticina.
3. Stem rust — https://www.fao.org/4/y4011e/y4011e0g.htm
   "The wheat rusts" — FAO. Cause: Puccinia graminis f. sp. tritici.
   NOTE: the UC IPM page "Stem Rusts of Wheat, Barley, and Oats"
   (https://ipm.ucanr.edu/agriculture/small-grains/stem-rusts-of-wheat-barley-and-oats/)
   was also opened (brick-red elongated pustules with tattered edges, 7–15 day
   onset, volunteer small grains as inoculum) but did not state the forma
   specialis, so the FAO chapter — which gives it explicitly — is the record's
   cited source.
4. Powdery mildew — https://extensionpubs.unl.edu/publication/g1997/2010/html/view
   "Powdery Mildew of Wheat (G1997)" — University of Nebraska–Lincoln Extension.
   Cause: Blumeria graminis f. sp. tritici.
5. Fusarium head blight — https://pnwhandbooks.org/plantdisease/host-disease/wheat-triticum-aestivum-fusarium-head-blight-scab
   "Wheat (Triticum aestivum)-Fusarium Head Blight (Scab)" — Pacific Northwest
   Pest Management Handbooks (rev. March 2026). Cause: Fusarium graminearum
   (among other Fusarium spp.).
6. Septoria tritici blotch — https://pnwhandbooks.org/plantdisease/host-disease/wheat-triticum-aestivum-septoria-tritici-blotch
   "Wheat (Triticum aestivum)-Septoria tritici Blotch" — Pacific Northwest Pest
   Management Handbooks (rev. March 2026). Cause: Zymoseptoria tritici.
7. Tan spot — https://cropprotectionnetwork.org/encyclopedia/tan-spot-of-wheat
   "Tan Spot of Wheat" — Crop Protection Network. Cause: Pyrenophora
   tritici-repentis.
8. Wheat streak mosaic virus — https://extension.sdstate.edu/keep-watch-wheat-streak-mosaic-virus-developing-south-dakota-fields
   "Keep Watch: Wheat Streak Mosaic Virus is Developing in South Dakota Fields" —
   South Dakota State University Extension. Vector: wheat curl mite
   (Aceria tosichella).
9. Take-all — https://cropprotectionnetwork.org/encyclopedia/take-all-of-wheat
   "Take-All of Wheat" — Crop Protection Network. Cause: Gaeumannomyces
   graminis var. tritici (page also names var. avenae).
