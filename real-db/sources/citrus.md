# Citrus (حمضيات) — sources, batch of 2026-10-24

10 records, all pages actually opened and read in this run. Distinct
URLs: 9 — the UF/IFAS citrus disease guide (CH159) is cited for two
records (melanose and Alternaria brown spot), each from its own section
of that guide; every other record has its own page.

1. Huanglongbing (citrus greening) — https://ipm.ucanr.edu/agriculture/citrus/huanglongbing/
   "Huanglongbing / Citrus / Agriculture: Pest Management Guidelines" —
   UC Statewide IPM Program (UC IPM). Cause: Candidatus Liberibacter
   asiaticus (the pathogen currently found in the US, per the page).
2. Phytophthora root rot — https://ipm.ucanr.edu/agriculture/citrus/phytophthora-root-rot/
   "Phytophthora Root Rot / Citrus" — UC IPM. Cause named in the record:
   Phytophthora citrophthora (winter/summer root rot on the page;
   P. parasitica is the warm-weather species also named there).
3. Brown rot of citrus fruit — https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/brown-rot-of-citrus/
   "Brown Rot Of Citrus" — UC IPM. Cause: several Phytophthora species
   (record uses Phytophthora spp., as the page names no single species).
4. Citrus canker — https://agriculture.vic.gov.au/biosecurity/plant-diseases/fruit-and-nut-diseases/citrus-canker
   "Citrus canker" — Agriculture Victoria (Victorian Government).
   Cause: Xanthomonas citri subsp. citri.
5. Citrus black spot — https://gd.eppo.int/taxon/GUIGCI/datasheet
   "EPPO Datasheet: Phyllosticta citricarpa" — EPPO Global Database.
   Cause: Phyllosticta citricarpa (syn. Guignardia citricarpa).
   EPPO code GUIGCI (shown on the opened page). Symptom detail is
   cross-checked in the UF/IFAS guide (CH159) section on black spot,
   but the cited source_url is the EPPO datasheet.
6. Greasy spot — https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/HS263
   "HS-1016/HS263: Field Identification and Management of Greasy Spot
   Disease" — UF/IFAS Extension. Cause: Zasmidium citrigriseum,
   formerly Mycosphaerella citri.
7. Melanose — https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/CH159
   "HS-798/CH159: A Guide to Citrus Disease Identification" — UF/IFAS
   Extension, melanose section. Cause: Diaporthe citri.
8. Alternaria brown spot — https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/CH159
   Same UF/IFAS guide, Alternaria brown spot section.
   Cause: Alternaria alternata. The UF/IFAS production guide
   (https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/CG021, Alternaria Brown Spot)
   was also opened for cultivar/host and weather-threshold detail;
   the record's cited source_url is CH159, which carries the symptom
   description.
9. Bacterial blast (black pit) — https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/bacterial-blast-of-citrus/
   "Bacterial Blast of Citrus / Home and Landscape" — UC IPM.
   Cause: Pseudomonas syringae.
10. Stubborn disease — https://ipm.ucanr.edu/agriculture/citrus/stubborn-disease/
    "Stubborn Disease / Citrus / Agriculture: Pest Management Guidelines"
    — UC IPM. Cause: Spiroplasma citri.

Opened but NOT counted:
- UC IPM Phytophthora Gummosis (https://ipm.ucanr.edu/agriculture/citrus/phytophthora-gummosis/):
  the page names no Phytophthora species, so a separate gummosis record
  would have collided with the brown-rot record's Phytophthora spp.
  key and added no distinct scientific name. Root rot and brown rot
  already cover the genus on citrus in this batch.
- UF/IFAS citrus disease guide sections on citrus scab and postbloom
  fruit drop were read, but no separate record was made: scab's
  pathogen name is not given in the guide section itself, and
  postbloom fruit drop's agent is not named as a distinct species
  there either — making a record would have required borrowing a name
  from another page, against SPEC rule zero.

EPPO codes: only black spot (GUIGCI). All other records carry
eppo_code "" — never invented.
Nothing dropped for lack of a source among the 10 counted diseases.
